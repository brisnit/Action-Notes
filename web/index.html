How it works

From a lesson to a lived experience — in three moves.

Action Notes wraps your existing content with the structure people need to stay engaged, reflect honestly, and actually apply what they're learning.

1

Upload or stream your content

Sermon, lecture, podcast episode, live event recording — drop it in and we structure it.

2

Add interactive moments

Reflections, quick checks, action items, discussion prompts — generated for you or written by hand.

3

Watch your community grow

Track streaks, completion, and reflection quality. Celebrate progress together.

Interactive content

Every lesson becomes a conversation, not a download.

Add reflections, polls, comprehension checks, and action steps right inside the listening experience. Participants engage instead of zoning out.

  • ✓ Open‑ended reflections with private notes
  • ✓ Multiple choice and scaled response questions
  • ✓ Action items participants commit to and check off later
  • ✓ Discussion prompts that flow into small‑group conversation
